About Safena Hotel

Nestled in the heart of Skardu Valley, Safena Hotel offers a perfect blend of comfort, elegance, and warm Balti hospitality. With a stellar 4.7 rating from 57+ reviews, we take pride in providing an unforgettable stay experience. Overlooking the majestic Karakoram Range, we offer modern amenities, delicious cuisine, and breathtaking mountain views.

Conveniently located in Skardu city center, we are your gateway to the stunning landscapes of Gilgit-Baltistan. Whether you're here for adventure or relaxation, Safena Hotel ensures a memorable stay.
